## Lessons Learned

- Repeated watchlist names should be filtered by persistence plus liquidity quality, otherwise the scanner keeps surfacing the same weak-volume pullbacks.
- Protection maintenance must distrust empty aggregate order snapshots and re-check per symbol before assuming protection is missing.
- Access-path failures should short-circuit downstream maintenance so one env-file read error does not fan out into duplicated protection noise.
- When live slots are full, the service should optimize for position defense and review quality instead of treating the hour as an entry-generation cycle.
