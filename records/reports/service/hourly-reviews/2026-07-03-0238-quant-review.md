## Lessons Learned

- Repeated watchlist names should be filtered by persistence plus liquidity quality, otherwise the scanner keeps surfacing the same weak-volume pullbacks.
- Weak-volume candidates should stay in review unless follow-through volume confirms; do not treat a single high score as fresh momentum.
- Margin-insufficient rejections are sizing/capacity failures; cool down new entries and tighten the available-margin gate before retrying.
- When live slots are full, the service should optimize for position defense and review quality instead of treating the hour as an entry-generation cycle.
- After live entry rejection or timeout (margin_insufficient), the service should cool down new entries while keeping active-position protection maintenance running.
